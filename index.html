The Story

Who I Am

I grew up in Gujarat, India — served as Class President for four straight years at Dharmsinh Desai University, not because it looked good on paper, but because I'm wired to build structure where there isn't any.

I moved to Boston in 2024 to do a Master's at Northeastern, and promptly started doing two jobs at once: building healthcare AI pipelines in production on GCP while designing and teaching two data engineering courses from scratch to 80+ graduate students.

My identity is simple: I build systems that work after the demo ends. Every project has validation, observability, and a clear answer to "what happens when this breaks at 2am?" That's what separates data engineering from data science theater.

Right now I'm in an intense job search — I graduate in May 2026 and I'm looking for a team that builds data infrastructure or applied AI seriously. If that's you, let's talk.

⬡ Ship before polish
A deployed pipeline with rough edges beats a perfect notebook. I bias toward production — Cloud Run, Airflow DAGs, Kubernetes — not localhost demos.
⬡ Validation is not optional
Schema validation, PII controls, audit trails, DVC versioning. Data without quality guarantees is just expensive noise.
⬡ Teach what you know
I designed and taught two graduate courses from scratch. Teaching forces clarity — if I can't explain it to 80 engineers, I don't understand it well enough.
⬡ Context-first systems
My AI systems maintain state, carry context forward, and generate follow-up questions — not one-shot answers. That's the difference between a chatbot and a tool.
